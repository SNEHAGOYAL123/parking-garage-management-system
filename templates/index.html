Smart Parking Garage Management

SpotSync helps parking attendants manage vehicle check-in, check-out, parking spots, EV availability, fees, and parking records from one simple system.

Key Features

Everything needed for a smooth daily parking workflow.

🚗 Vehicle Check-In

Quickly register arriving vehicles and automatically assign a suitable available parking spot.

💰 Accurate Fee Calculation

Calculate parking charges using hourly rates, part-hour rounding, and a daily maximum cap.

⚡ EV Spot Management

Track EV parking availability and ensure EV vehicles are assigned compatible EV spots.

🔎 Vehicle Search

Find parking records quickly using a vehicle's license plate number.

🅿️ Spot Tracking

Track compact, standard, and EV spots while preventing active spot double-booking.

📋 Parking Records

View parking history with pagination, sorting, status information, and timestamps.

Built for Parking Garage Operations

SpotSync is designed around the daily workflow of a parking garage.

👨‍💼 Parking Attendants

Check vehicles in and out, locate vehicles, monitor available spots, and handle parking charges.

🏢 Garage Operators

Monitor parking occupancy, maintain parking records, and manage different types of parking spots.

🚘 Drivers

Benefit from organized parking operations, compatible EV parking, and faster vehicle processing.

How SpotSync Helps

A simple system for managing the complete parking workflow.

🚗 Faster Check-In

Quickly assign an available compatible parking spot to an arriving vehicle.

💵 Correct Charges

Automatically calculate fees based on the vehicle's parking duration.

⚡ EV Visibility

Quickly check whether EV parking spots are currently available.

🔍 Quick Lookup

Search parking records by license plate instead of manually checking a large log.

What's Next?

Three possible improvements for future versions of SpotSync.

📊 Advanced Analytics

Add occupancy trends, revenue reports, peak-hour analysis, and other operational insights.

📱 Driver Notifications

Provide notifications for parking status, checkout information, and payment details.

🧭 Smart Spot Guidance

Guide attendants toward suitable available spots based on vehicle type and garage layout.
